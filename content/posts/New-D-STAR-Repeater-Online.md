The Islanders Amateur Radio Group at Hilton Head Island installed a new D-STAR repeater on the Island this week.  Congratulations to Eddie King, N4HEK, the repeater trustee and the team in Hilton Head/Bluffton.  Also, a special thanks to Rick, N1RCW, for his Allstar expertise. 

The D-STAR repeater is connected to our URF517A Reflector giving us wider D-STAR RF coverage and more fun!

The fulltime Allstar node is now 50076 and for Echolink N4HEK-R.  A connection to the Bluffton 440 machine will be made for Nets.

See [Connecting to RadioSC](https://radiosc.net/posts/connecting-to-radiosc/) and [Quadnet Digital Services and RadioSC](https://radiosc.net/quadnet-and-radiosc/) for more connection details.
