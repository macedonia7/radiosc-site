The Ham.Live Net Logging software is shutting down at the end of June 2026. The developer has kindly make his software [open source software](https://github.com/Constant-Digital-Holdings-LLC/hamlive-oss) using the MIT license.

As a result the [Colorado Digital Group](https://coloradodigital.net/) has installed an instance of the net logging software and have made it available for use.  They have also started making improvements.

The RadioSC-based nets that were formerly on ham.live will now be hosted on [netcontrol.live](https://netcontrol.live/).  Please join us there for future nets starting on June 22, 2026.
