When the Ham.Live Net Logging software shutdown at the end of June 2026. The developer has kindly make Open Sourced his [software](https://github.com/Constant-Digital-Holdings-LLC/hamlive-oss) using the MIT license.

As a result the [Colorado Digital Group](https://coloradodigital.net/) installed an instance of the net logging software and have made it available for use.  They also started making improvements.

The RadioSC-based nets used this new software for a few weeks with good results.  Netcontrol.live is a good system and many are using it.

However, recently a local ham, W2ADX, created the [CQ-QSO logging software](https://cq-qso.com/) also based upon ham.live.  This new software has some infrastructure benefits and statistical reporting that we see as important for our needs and since the developer is local we expect that improvements will be more straightforward to implement. Therefore beginning on August 6th, 2026 we will move over to the [CQ-QSO](https://cq-qso.com/) logging software.  Please join us there for all our future nets.
